# Claim Document Scope Audit

`docs/claims/current_claim_lock.md` and `docs/release/final_project_claim.md`
remain historical/current Generation 1 research-track documents. Gate 28A
does not rewrite them and does not silently promote their claims to the new
Generation 2 program.

The Generation 2 wording is defined separately in
`docs/claims/generation2_claim_policy.md`. A future transition document must
explicitly identify which evidence supports any change in claim scope.
