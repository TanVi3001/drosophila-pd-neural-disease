# Public Abstract

## English

This project reports a closed prospective computational validation track for a
driver-defined Parkin neural perturbation in a Drosophila brain-body runtime.
The preregistered virtual prediction did not support the expected locomotor
impairment direction. Held-out biological Parkin evidence supported
locomotor/climbing impairment. Because climbing and planar speed are
non-equivalent assays, the final result is
`DIRECTIONAL_CROSS_ASSAY_DISCORDANCE`, not quantitative cross-assay
validation. The scientific result is `NEGATIVE_VALIDATION_RESULT`.

The current evidence does not support biological Parkinson validation,
gene-specific biological validation, clinical validation, pharmacological
efficacy, or successful Parkin phenotype replication.

## Tieng Viet

Du an bao cao mot track validation tinh toan tien cuu da dong voi perturbation
than kinh Parkin duoc dinh nghia boi driver trong runtime nao-co-the ruoi
giam. Du doan mo phong da khoa khong ung ho huong suy giam van dong da dang ky
truoc. Bang chung sinh hoc holdout ung ho suy giam van dong/climbing, nhung
climbing va planar speed la hai assay khong tuong duong. Vi vay ket qua cuoi
cung la `DIRECTIONAL_CROSS_ASSAY_DISCORDANCE` va
`NEGATIVE_VALIDATION_RESULT`, khong phai quantitative cross-assay validation.

## Historical Chen/Pozo track

The earlier Chen calibration and Pozo PINK1 holdout remain valid historical
evidence. That track reported directional computational concordance together
with a substantial quantitative ratio mismatch. It is not the current Parkin
Gate24E conclusion.

## Scientific boundary

This abstract does not claim biological Parkinson validation, a gene-specific
model, clinical validation, drug validation, or a replacement for wet-lab
experiments. English boundary: `not biological validation`, `not gene-specific
validation`, `not clinical validation`, and `not drug validation`.
