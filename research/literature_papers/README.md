# Literature papers used by the project

Thu muc nay luu cac paper nguon co quyen phan phoi lai ro rang va metadata
cho paper chi duoc tham chieu. Cac PDF duoc giu nguyen ban, khong chen nhan
xet vao noi dung goc. Nhan xet cua nhom nam trong `research/paper_review/`.

## PDF duoc commit

- `open_access/godena_2014_lrrk2_microtubule.pdf`: CC BY 4.0.
- `open_access/hwang_2013_dj1_dlp.pdf`: Creative Commons Attribution License.
- `open_access/pokrzywa_2017_alpha_syn_flytracker.pdf`: Creative Commons Attribution License.
- `open_access/pozo_2022_pink1_serotonin.pdf`: CC BY 4.0.

Mỗi file co SHA256 va source URL trong `source_manifest.json`. Khi dung hinh,
bang, hoac so lieu tu paper, can trich dan tac gia, DOI va nguon goc theo yeu
cau cua paper, khong chi trich dan repository nay.

## Riemensperger 2011

`riemensperger_2011_dopamine_deficiency.pdf` khong duoc commit. Ban PDF cuc
bo va trang PMC deu cong khai de doc, nhung ban PDF da kiem tra khong neu giay
phep Creative Commons hay quyen phan phoi lai ro rang. Repository chi luu
metadata, SHA256 cua ban cuc bo da review va link PMC/DOI trong manifest.

Nguon doc chinh thuc:

- PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3021077/
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1010930108

Neu nhom co van ban cho phep phan phoi lai hoac mot ban co license ro rang,
co the them PDF vao sau va cap nhat manifest bang mot commit moi.

## Pham vi su dung

Nam paper nay la evidence literature cho cac phenotype va assay khac nhau.
Chung khong phai mot dataset dong nhat va khong duoc gop thanh mot calibration
loss. Quy tac assay transfer, calibration, holdout va claim lock cua project
van co hieu luc.
